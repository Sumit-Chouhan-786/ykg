About YKG Professional Corporation

YKG Professional Corporation (YKG PC) is a Canadian accounting and advisory firm founded by Yogesh Gupta, CPA (Ontario), CA (India). We serve Canadian entrepreneurs, professionals, and families with end-to-end accounting, tax, and advisory services — from routine compliance to strategic planning and valuation.

We also bring specialised expertise for clients with India–Canada cross-border interests, helping them navigate tax planning, remittances, business structuring, and financial advisory on both sides of the border.

With over 15 years of experience in banking, corporate finance, project lending, and senior leadership roles, Yogesh has:

  • Closed 400M+ CAD in debt, equity, and real estate transactions;
  • Managed complex treasury, IFRS reporting, and compliance functions;
  • Delivered solutions that combine Canadian rigour with global insight.

Career Highlights

Chief Financial Officer – Clarion Properties
Deputy CFO – Country Garden Holdings (India)
Structured Finance Leadership – Edelweiss Financial, Yes Bank, L&T Infrastructure
Investment Banking & Credit – ICICI Bank

Since relocating to Canada in 2022, Yogesh has earned his CPA Ontario designation, is advancing through the CBV Program, and is enrolled in CPA Canada’s In-Depth Tax Program to deepen his tax and valuation expertise.

Our Logo Story

The YKG Professional Corporation logo is defined by two graceful arcs forming a bridge above the firm’s name.

  • The red arc represents Canada — stability, trust, and opportunity in our adopted home.
  • The orange arc represents India — heritage, resilience, and the roots of a long professional journey.

Together, these arcs symbolise the distance between two homes — a distance bridged by shared values, ethics, and integrity. The deep blue lettering reinforces reliability and professionalism.

Designed with care by a close friend who prefers to remain anonymous, the logo is a personal emblem of bridging worlds through trusted advisory services.
