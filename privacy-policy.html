🔒 Privacy Policy — YKG Professional Corporation (CPAYKG)

Last updated: November 2025

YKG Professional Corporation (“YKG PC”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and the professional obligations of Chartered Professional Accountants in Ontario.

1. Scope of Policy

This policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information obtained through our website (cpaykg.ca), emails, forms, and professional engagements.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, and postal address.
  • Inquiry details: messages submitted through forms or emails.
  • Client data: financial and tax information provided for professional engagements (subject to confidentiality obligations).
  • Website usage data: device information, IP address, browser type, and analytics statistics via Google Tag Manager or similar tools.
3. Purpose of Collection

We collect and use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • Deliver accounting, tax, valuation, and advisory services.
  • Fulfil legal and regulatory obligations under CRA and CPA Ontario rules.
  • Improve our website and service quality.
  • Communicate relevant updates, subject to consent.
4. Consent

By providing your personal information, you consent to its collection and use as described in this policy. You may withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions, by contacting us.

5. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose information only to:

  • Authorized employees and contractors bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Service providers who assist in hosting, email, or data processing (with appropriate safeguards).
  • Regulatory or tax authorities when required by law or professional duty (e.g., CRA or CPA Ontario).
  • Other parties with your explicit consent.
6. Safeguards

We employ administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect personal information against loss, theft, unauthorized access, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes identified or to comply with professional and legal requirements, including record-retention rules under CPA Ontario and CRA.

8. Access and Correction

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to professional retention requirements and applicable law.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools (e.g., Google Tag Manager) to analyze traffic and improve functionality. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, although some features may not function properly.

10. Third-Party Links

Links to external websites are provided for convenience only. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on our website with the new effective date.
