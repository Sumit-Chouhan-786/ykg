Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

YKG Professional Corporation (“CPAYKG”, “we”, “our”) is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with:

  • PIPEDA — Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act
  • CPA Ontario’s Rules of Professional Conduct
  • CRA confidentiality and taxpayer-rights rules
  • CBV Institute Standards (where valuation is involved)

1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Contact details (name, email, phone)
  • Inquiry information via contact forms
  • Client-provided tax, accounting, and financial documents
  • Website analytics via Google Analytics

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Perform tax, accounting, valuation, and advisory engagements
  • Meet CRA, CPA Ontario, and CBV Institute requirements
  • Improve our website and client experience

3. Disclosure

We do not sell or rent information. Disclosure occurs only:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To service providers under confidentiality
  • If legally required (CRA, court orders, CPA Ontario)

4. Safeguards

We use administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

5. Retention

Records are retained in accordance with CPA Ontario, CRA, and CBV Institute guidelines.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information at any time.
