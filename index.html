Our Logo Story

The YKG Professional Corporation logo is defined by two graceful arcs forming a bridge above the firm’s name.

  • The red arc represents Canada — stability, trust, and opportunity in our adopted home.
  • The orange arc represents India — heritage, resilience, and the roots of a long professional journey.

Together, these arcs symbolise the distance between two homes — a distance bridged by shared values, ethics, and integrity. The deep blue lettering reinforces reliability and professionalism.

Designed with care by a close friend who prefers to remain anonymous, the logo is a personal emblem of bridging worlds through trusted advisory services.
